I am a dual-degree graduate student at **Purdue University** specializing in the intersection of **Autonomous Systems** and **Technology Management**. My academic path combines a **Master of Science in Interdisciplinary Engineering (MSe)** with a **Master of Business and Technology (MBT)**, driven by a goal to bridge the gap between *advanced robotics research* and *scalable industry applications*.

My work focuses on the development of intelligent, embodied agents. I am interested in how robotcs perceive and navigate complex environments. By leveraging high-performance computing and learning-based frameworks, I build systems designed for reliability and physical world interaction. 

For the past four years, I have served as a **Teaching Assistant** within **The Data Mine Corporate Partners Program** at Purdue. In this role, I act as a bridge between student technical teams and Corporate Partners, guiding the translation of raw data and complex engineering challenges into strategic value. I am passionate about mentorship and the collaborative process of bringing technical projects to life.

Outside of the lab, I enjoy weight lifting, hiking and writing.My long-term vision is to lead at the frontier of robotics—whether through academia, research science, or entrepreneurial ventures.
